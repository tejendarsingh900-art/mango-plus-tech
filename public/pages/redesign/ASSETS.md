# Public page redesign assets

All seven PNG originals were generated using the built-in image-generation tool for this project. WebP files are optimized website copies. Team/consultant photographs are illustrative generated imagery.

Prompt briefs:

- `engagement-new-build`: built-in image generation with genuine transparent alpha; premium white/cobalt 3D browser evolving from wireframe to finished page, small blue launch rocket and modular glass interface blocks. Isolated complete objects, no backdrop or floor, padded 4:3 composition. Used in the new-project engagement card.
- `engagement-upgrade`: built-in image generation with genuine transparent alpha; premium white/cobalt 3D analytics browser, blue magnifying glass, rising arrow and adjustment-sliders card. Isolated complete objects, no backdrop or floor, padded 4:3 composition. Used in the existing-experience improvement card.

- `service-benefits-background`: built-in image generation, wide airy ice-blue/white background for the service-page benefits section. Translucent blue glass ribbon at lower edges, faint frosted arches upper right and restrained luminous side details, with a clean low-detail centre for readable navy text and white cards. No text, logos or foreground objects. PNG original retained; optimized WebP used as a cover background.

- `services`: isolated transparent white/cobalt 3D browser, mobile screen, code tile, database and growth elements; no floor or enclosing background.
- `company`: four Indian creative agency professionals collaborating around a laptop in a bright sunlit studio, white/blue clothing, natural business photography.
- `work`: isolated transparent portfolio collage with ecommerce, SaaS and furniture browser designs and smartphone, white/cobalt materials.
- `insights`: isolated transparent article cards with search, analytics and responsive-design illustrations, notebook, blue magnifier and lightbulb.
- `careers`: five Indian technology/creative professionals discussing sketches near a whiteboard in a bright contemporary studio.
- `contact`: transparent realistic Indian woman consultant in royal-blue blazer, holding a laptop and making an inviting open-hand gesture; no cropped hands or office backdrop.
- `commerce`: isolated transparent storefront browser with home products and blue ceramics, smartphone, checkout card and parcel.

Generated assets are used by the public page heroes and supporting service sections. Public pages share blue typography, navigation, footer and responsive layouts. Homepage sections deliberately use different native gradients, dot/grid patterns and white surfaces instead of repeating one background image.

Contact enquiries retain the existing `mailto:` action; newsletter forms hand off to the contact page. This visual redesign does not add or verify a backend mail-delivery service.
